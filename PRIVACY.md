# Color Door Rush — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 22, 2026

Color Door Rush ("the app") is published by surproductions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what
information the app handles. The short version: **the app does not collect, transmit or share any
personal information.**

## Information we collect
None. The app has no accounts and requires no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising,
crash-reporting or tracking software, and it makes no network requests.

## Information stored on your device
To make the game work, the app saves the following **only on your device**, using Apple's standard
local storage (UserDefaults):

- game progress: best scores, run statistics, recent run history, achievements and unlocked cosmetics
- in-game currency ("shards") earned by playing
- your settings: sound, music, haptics, controls and accessibility preferences
- whether you have completed the tutorial

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. If your device makes backups
(iCloud or computer backups), Apple's backup services may include this data under Apple's own
privacy terms. You can erase it at any time with **Settings → Reset Progress** in the app, or by
deleting the app.

## Tracking
The app does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

## Children's privacy
The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from
children.

## Purchases and ads
The app has no in-app purchases and no advertisements. Shards are earned only through gameplay
and have no real-world value.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective
date. Changes will also be included with the app update that introduces them.
