Your orb is racing down a neon highway, and every gate is a split-second choice. Match your color, dodge the barriers, and see how far you can rush.

Color Gate Rush is a fast, one-thumb arcade game that's easy to learn and hard to put down. Runs are short, retries are instant, and every attempt pushes you a little further.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the left or right side of the screen to switch lanes, or swipe if you prefer.
• Fly only through gates that match your orb's color and shape.
• Avoid the striped barriers.
• Hit a prism and your orb changes color, so plan your next move fast.
• Grab shards to unlock new looks, and catch a rare shield to survive one mistake.

FEATURES
• Five difficulty tiers, from Warm-Up to Prismatic. New colors join the mix and the pace keeps climbing.
• Every course is generated fresh and always beatable. If you crash, it was your call.
• Combo multipliers up to x5 and milestone bonuses for chasing high scores.
• 15 achievements to earn.
• 17 cosmetics to unlock: orbs, trails and color themes.
• An interactive tutorial that teaches you in under a minute.
• Synth soundtrack, punchy sound effects and haptic feedback.

PLAYABLE BY EVERYONE
• Every color has its own shape, so you never have to rely on color alone.
• Color Assist labels every gate by name.
• Reduce Motion turns off screen shake and speed effects.
• Plays fully offline.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking.
